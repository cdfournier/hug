HUG · Round 2 · Refining 1a

Held, four ways

Same idea as 1a, with the proportions changed one at a time: the size of the dot, how far the arc wraps, and whether the pair leans. Compare them at 16px. That size decides which one survives as an app icon.

2aBalanced

The original 1a, centered optically. The dot and the arc carry equal weight.

2bThe one held

A bigger dot inside a lighter arc. The Agent becomes the subject, and the holding is quieter.

2cLeaning in

The pair tilts, as if the dot is resting its head. It's the most human of the four, but less of a static symbol.

2dFully wrapped

The arc climbs higher around the dot. It feels more sheltered, a nod to "home" and the hut reference in the brief.

HUG · Symbol exploration · Round 1

Three ways to hold

Three standalone symbols built on the embrace idea, in Special Violet. Each is drawn with one stroke weight and round caps, to match the line icons in the concept screens. Each is shown as an app icon, on the dark canvas, on light, at small sizes, and with the wordmark.

1a Held

One form, cradled by another. The Agent is the dot and the Operator is the arc around it. It reads as "you are known" without needing a face.
